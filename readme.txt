Search engine and AI visibility optimisation for WordPress, built for speed: nothing added to your visitors' pages, and a guided setup.

== Description ==

Zinn® SEO is search and AI visibility for WordPress, built by Neil Lock (25+ years in SEO) for sites from one page to a million posts.

Your pages stay as fast as they were: no front-end script or stylesheet (a few hundred bytes of style where you use a block), and no comment or generator tag in your source.

= Titles, descriptions and robots for every page =

* Title and description templates for every content type, archive and special page, with variables and custom fields.
* A live preview on a real page while you edit a template, with the length search engines show.
* Every post, page and term can set its own title, description, canonical and robots.
* Each author's page too: title, description, sharing card, canonical, robots and a 301, on the profile.
* Click a suggested title or description to edit it.
* Hide a whole content type from search results, or one page; limit snippets and image or video previews. Archives and second pages get the right canonical address.
* Site verification for Google, Bing, Yandex, Baidu, Pinterest and Naver.
* Google Discover readiness, with a checklist on every post.

= Social sharing =

* Open Graph and X (Twitter) cards on every page, with the best image picked for you.
* Mastodon and fediverse author credit, Pinterest "nopin", and the author's name for LinkedIn.
* **Pro:** branded social cards drawn once on your server: the title over your colours or the post's image, with your logo, right to left where needed.

= Bulk editing =

* Edit titles, descriptions, indexing and canonical addresses of thousands of posts and terms in one table, filtered by what is missing.
* Change every matching page in one go, and undo any change later.
* Page types, FAQs and how-to steps for many pages from one table.
* **Pro:** every SEO field of every post and term to a CSV file for Excel or Google Sheets and back, previewed before anything changes.

= Sitemaps that do not crash big sites =

* XML sitemaps written ahead as files: a million posts in small steps with bounded memory; an edit rebuilds only its file.
* Honest "last changed" dates: a save that changes nothing a reader sees keeps the old date.
* Leave out types or pages; noindex, private and canonicalised pages leave by themselves.
* Every language with Tranzly: per-language sitemap files and hreflang alternates for each page.
* A page of links for people: the `[zinn_seo_sitemap]` shortcode.
* Readable in every browser, never stored by page caches.
* **Pro:** videos in the sitemap, a Google News sitemap of the last two days, and one sitemap index for a multisite network.

= robots.txt, AI crawlers and llms.txt =

* A robots.txt editor with a tester; an existing file's rules can be brought in.
* Choose which AI crawlers may read your site; one click for "allow answers, block training".
* Content signals in robots.txt and the TDM reservation file (EU), behind a switch.
* llms.txt and llms-full.txt for AI assistants, from your key pages and newest posts; with Tranzly, one per language.
* **Pro:** a Markdown version of every page for AI agents (`/page.md`), never indexed as a duplicate; and a log of which AI crawlers visit and what they read.

= Indexing =

* IndexNow: new, changed and removed pages sent to Bing, Yandex, Seznam, Naver and others at once, with retries and a log. Off until switched on.
* "Inspect in Google" under every post opens Search Console's URL inspection, where Request indexing is.
* Staging copies are recognised and kept out of search by themselves.

= Structured data: one graph per page =

* Every page prints ONE schema.org graph: site, owner, page, article, author, breadcrumbs and, with Tranzly, its languages.
* Authors become one person with their credentials and profiles (E-E-A-T), TikTok included, for authors and for the site.
* Your blocks, WooCommerce products and Page Builder Sandwich blocks join that graph; with another SEO plugin active, they join its graph.
* An offline check of any page's structured data against schema.org and Google's current rules.
* AI fills FAQs and structured data site-wide.
* A site graph for AI agents at `/.well-known/site-graph.json`, listed in llms.txt.
* **Pro:** recipes, events, jobs, courses, software, books, reviews, services, videos and FAQs from a form; any schema.org type designed visually; types by rule.

= SEO blocks for every page builder =

* Quick Answer (the short answer AI assistants quote), table of contents, key takeaways, FAQ, how-to, pros and cons, review, author box, related content, local business and breadcrumbs.
* Each block works in the block editor, Page Builder Sandwich, Elementor and as a `[zinn_seo]` shortcode, with its structured data in the page's graph. No block loads a script.
* Elementor: an SEO section with a live search preview, and dynamic tags.
* **Pro:** comparison tables, fact checks, a linked glossary, video that loads nothing until played, and blocks you design yourself.

= Content analysis of the page visitors see =

* Live SEO and readability checks in every editor, measured on the page as shown, not the saved markup.
* Focus phrases with synonyms and word forms (three per page; unlimited with Pro), related phrases and stricter cornerstone checks.
* Readability in every language WordPress ships; transition words, passive voice and reading ease in 30+ languages; inclusive language in 12.
* Accessibility checks: heading order, "click here" links, missing alt text, untitled frames.
* Search previews, scores in post lists and a duplicate finder.
* **Pro:** answer-readiness (quotable by AI answers), search intent and topic coverage against the pages that rank; SEO revisions with who, when and one-click restore.

= Internal links =

* Link suggestions while you write, from your own site, inserted with one click; a link index with orphans, dead ends and link counts.
* Link rules (nofollow, sponsored, new tab) by site or cloaked path; `zinn_seo_breadcrumbs()` for themes.
* Breadcrumb rules per content type, and shorter trails with `max` and `show_home`.
* **Pro:** every suggestion, "Add link" from pages that should link here, suggestions by meaning with your own AI key, automatic links, and a broken link checker with e-mail alerts.

= Fix it and the SEO inspector =

* One prioritised list of what to fix across the site, with time estimates and one-click fixes.
* An "SEO" admin-bar item showing what each live page prints. Visitors get nothing.

= Redirects, 404s and protection =

* Redirects answered before your theme loads, as fast with 100,000 as with ten.
* Automatic redirects for moved and deleted pages; chain fixer; imports from six plugins.
* A 404 monitor that cannot fill your database; safe permalink and domain changes.
* A hacked-site SEO spam scan and site search insights.
* **Pro:** redirects before any plugin loads, in .htaccess or as Nginx, Apache and Caddy rules; SEO lock; weekly spam watch.

= Rankings, Google index and AI answers: pay only for what you run =

* Connect a Zinn® account in two clicks. No subscription: each check shows its price first; no answer, no charge.
* Rank tracker by country and device; Google index checker; whether ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini and Claude recommend you; backlink watch; a site audit; keyword research.
* Research toolbox: a street-by-street map grid, review alerts, competitor traffic, People also ask as an FAQ.
* Low credit and paused checks are shown in WordPress and e-mailed.
* **Pro:** research per country, competitor gaps, a local rank grid, brand mentions, a disavow file, negative-SEO alerts, Shopping, Amazon and YouTube ranks, briefs and AI review replies.

= Google Search Console, Bing and Analytics, inside WordPress =

* Sign in to Google once: clicks, impressions and position for every page and search, which pages Google has indexed and why not, and your sitemap sent to Google and Bing for you. Free.
* Real-visitor page speed for the site and each template, with what slows it and the fix.
* **Pro:** the searches each page nearly ranks for and the exact change (AI rewrite in one click), pages losing clicks, topics to write next, changes on your clicks chart, title tests with the winner kept, revenue from search (no script added), crawl budget, Cloudflare edge redirects, Google reviews and posts, Apple and Bing listings.

= AI that writes, you approve =

* Use your own key (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google Gemini and more, or a model on your own server), or pay as you go with Zinn® credits.
* Titles, descriptions, image alt text and structured data for any page. Nothing changes until you use it.
* When AI fails you are told why, and what to do.
* **Pro:** the whole site at once, FAQs, product copy, internal links, redirects for 404s and site moves, content briefs and "ask your SEO data".

= Guided setup =

* Recognises your kind of site and your other plugins, and asks only what it cannot work out.
* Moves you over from Yoast SEO, Rank Math, All in One SEO, SEOPress and five more: every page compared, the old plugin switched off only when they match.
* Every step can be skipped or run again later.
* Warns you when the site is asking search engines not to index it, and fixes it with one switch.

= Built for speed and for big sites =

* Nothing added to your visitors' pages, and no admin script on any screen except its own.
* SEO data is stored with each post and term: no second index table to rebuild.
* Long jobs run in resumable steps.
* Works with 50+ plugins (see the FAQ); one you do not use costs nothing.
* Runs on PHP 7.4 to 8.5 and WordPress 6.4 and newer.

= No-trace mode =

Hide that the site uses an SEO plugin: no plugin names in addresses, no sitemap stylesheet, neutral ids in structured data. Switching lists every address that would change.

= Your data =

Deleting the plugin removes every setting, field, table and role it created, unless you choose to keep your data.

**Help and support from Zinn Digital® (off until you use it).** Get help → Connect sends the email and name you type, the plugin's name and version, this site's address and title, WordPress, PHP and your language to `https://api.zinndigital.com/v1/plugin-support/connections` (an encrypted token is kept; Disconnect deletes it). A request sends what you type, your language and your licence's plan to `/v1/plugin-support/tickets`, plus site details only if you tick "Include site details".

**Rankings and checks from Zinn Digital® (off until you connect).** After you tick "I agree", SEO → Credit sends this site's address, an install id, plugin and WordPress versions and your language (and the email and name you type, to create an account) to `https://api.zinndigital.com/v1/seo-connect/start`, `/v1/seo-connect/signup` and `/v1/seo-connect/token`; you approve in a new tab; an encrypted token is kept. Disconnecting or deleting the plugin retires the token.

**Zinn® CDN (off until switched on).** Status, switching and purge: `https://api.zinndigital.com/v1/seo-connection/cdn` and `/v1/seo-connection/cdn/purge` (token only). While on, visitors load static files from Bunny.net, which sees their IP. [Terms](https://bunny.net/tos/), [privacy](https://bunny.net/privacy/).

**Google, through Zinn Digital® (off until you connect).** Google's sign-in opens in a new tab and Zinn Digital® keeps it. The site asks `https://api.zinndigital.com/v1/seo-connection/google…` for its own Search Console, Analytics (with AI-assistant visits and ad earnings) and Business Profile data, sends its sitemap address and — only for job or livestream pages you choose — tells Google they changed; page speed reads `/v1/seo-connection/crux`. [Google privacy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

**Site authority (off until connected).** Your SEO tool key is kept only at `https://api.zinndigital.com/v1/seo-connection/data-sources/`; scores: `/v1/seo-connection/data-history`, `/v1/seo-connection/data-targets`.

**Bing Webmaster Tools (off until you paste your key).** Calls `https://ssl.bing.com/webmaster/api.svc/json/` with your key to read your Bing data, send your sitemap and chosen pages, and (Pro) set the crawl rate. [Terms](https://www.microsoft.com/servicesagreement), [privacy](https://privacy.microsoft.com/privacystatement).

**Facebook (off until you add your own app).** Sends changed pages' addresses with your app's token to `https://graph.facebook.com/` for a fresh read. [Terms](https://developers.facebook.com/terms/), [privacy](https://www.facebook.com/privacy/policy/).

**Cloudflare (Pro, off until you paste a token).** Calls `https://api.cloudflare.com/client/v4/` to keep your redirects as Bulk Redirects, clear a changed page from the cache and set the AI-crawler rule. [Terms](https://www.cloudflare.com/website-terms/), [privacy](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/).

**IndexNow (off until you switch it on).** Sends the address of each page you publish, change or remove, with this site's host and its IndexNow key, to the service you choose (`https://api.indexnow.org/indexnow` by default, or Bing, Yandex, Seznam or Naver's `/indexnow`); never from a staging copy or a site closed to search engines. [IndexNow terms](https://www.indexnow.org/terms).

**Topic coverage (Pro, only when used).** Compare in the editor fetches the pages whose addresses you typed, at most daily, to read their subjects. Nothing about your site or users is sent.

**AI providers (off until you add a key).** Settings → AI connects the provider you choose with your own key (Google Gemini, OpenAI, Anthropic, Mistral, DeepSeek, OpenRouter or a compatible service). A feature sends only the text it needs (for alt text, the image), when you press its button or start a site-wide run. Each provider's terms apply.

**AI on Zinn® credits (off until chosen).** With a connected Zinn® account and Zinn® credits chosen under SEO → AI, that text (never an image) goes to `https://api.zinndigital.com/v1/plugin-ai/complete` instead, and your credit is debited.

**Recommended AI models list (off until ticked).** Settings → AI providers' daily check fetches the signed list from `https://api.zinndigital.com/v1/ai-model-catalogue`, sending nothing about your site. Connecting to Zinn® does not turn it on (in Pro it does).

**Writing tools (off until used):** your LanguageTool server (`/v2/check`); on credits `https://api.zinndigital.com/v1/seo-connection/images/` and `/v1/seo-connection/originality/` (sentences only); your Surfer or Frase key (`https://app.surferseo.com/api/v1/content_editors/`, `https://api.frase.io/api/v1/process_serp`).

**Agency tools (Pro, off until set up).** Client reports send the client's details, your brand and logos to `https://api.zinndigital.com/v1/seo-connection/reports/brand`, `/v1/seo-connection/reports/run`, `/v1/seo-connection/reports/sinks/` and `/v1/seo-connection/reports/portal-links/`, and daily counts of SEO work to `/v1/seo-connection/work-log`. Alerts go to the chat or webhook address you add; spreadsheet pages read the sheet you name.

**Wikidata (only when you ask).** Entity suggestions send the names AI found on the page to `https://www.wikidata.org/w/api.php`. Terms: https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Terms_of_Use · Privacy policy: https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Privacy_policy

**Other sites (only when you ask).** A competitor comparison or a site move reads the page or old sitemap you name.

"Inspect in Google/Bing" sends nothing. Nothing about visitors goes to any service above, except the CDN as said.

== Installation ==

1. In WordPress open Plugins → Add Plugin → Upload Plugin, choose the `zinn-seo.zip` file and press Install Now, then Activate.
2. Open **SEO** in the admin menu. The guided setup starts on its own.

= Does it slow my site down? =

No. It adds no script, no stylesheet and no comment to your visitors' pages, and loads one small setting per page. Its admin app loads only on its own screen.

= What happens when the Pro trial ends? =

The free 14-day trial needs no card. If it ends without a paid plan, the Pro features stop and the plugin carries on as the free plugin: your pages keep their titles, descriptions and redirects, and nothing breaks for visitors. Everything you set up with Pro is kept, so upgrading later brings it all back at once.

= Can it clean up malware and fix errors on my site? =

Yes, with Pro. SEO › Site care checks every WordPress, plugin and theme file against the official copies and your accounts, settings and pages for malware and spam, reads your error logs and explains each error with the fix, and cleans up the database safely. Every change is backed up first, nothing is deleted (only moved to quarantine), and everything can be undone in one click. The AI only sees code and log lines with your secrets removed, and a small code fix is switched on only after you read and approve it and it passes a test.

* SEO Manager and SEO Editor roles for colleagues who are not administrators; with Pro, permissions per role.
* Copy settings to another site with a file, previewing every change.
* Ctrl+K (Cmd+K) jumps to any setting, screen or post.
* Every setting and every page's SEO fields through the REST API, WP-CLI (`wp zinn-seo`) and AI assistants such as Claude and ChatGPT (MCP, off until you switch it on; changes tried first, approved by you if you want).
* Documented actions and filters, and `zinn_seo()` for themes and other plugins.
* Agency: white label, client logins, client reports and pages from a spreadsheet. Pro: tasks and chat alerts.

= Can AI assistants like Claude or ChatGPT work on my site? =

Let Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor or VS Code check and improve your SEO for you. Zinn® SEO is its own MCP server: switch it on under Settings → AI agents, and every action on its screens (300+ tools: titles and descriptions, structured data, redirects, sitemaps, robots.txt, llms.txt, audits, credit checks) is open to an assistant signed in as a WordPress user, with that user's permissions and nothing more. Claude and ChatGPT sign in by asking you to approve them; other apps use an application password.

* Off until you switch it on.
* Every change can be tried first: the assistant sees what would change and nothing is saved.
* Choose what happens to its changes: made straight away, held until you approve them on the AI agents tab, or never (read and suggest only).
* Every call is logged, and every change shows in the page's SEO history as made by an AI agent, ready to undo.
* "Optimise this site", a ready-made run: audit, plan, try each change, apply what you agree to, report.

= Can I run it beside another SEO plugin? =

Two SEO plugins on one site both print titles, descriptions and structured data, which confuses search engines. While another SEO plugin is active, Zinn® SEO leaves the page head to it (you can override this for testing under Search appearance). The setup tells you which other SEO plugin is active; keep it until its data has been brought into Zinn® SEO, then switch it off.

= How do I move from Yoast SEO, Rank Math or another SEO plugin? =

Open SEO → Move from another plugin. Count what would come over, copy it, then compare: every page is fetched as your current plugin shows it and set beside what Zinn® SEO would print. When the pages say the same, one button switches the other plugin off, reads the pages again and switches it back on if any of them changed. Nothing of the other plugin is deleted, and Undo takes everything back.

= What do the rank, index and AI checks cost? =

There is no subscription. You add credit to a Zinn® account and each check shows its price before it runs, from a fraction of a cent. The plugin shows your credit, how long it lasts at your current pace and warns you before it runs low. If a scheduled check cannot be paid for it waits, and carries on by itself when you top up: nothing is lost.

= Do I have to connect a Zinn® account? =

No. Everything else in the plugin works without one, and nothing is sent anywhere until you choose to connect.

= Does supporting so many plugins slow my site down? =

No. Zinn® SEO looks for a plugin by checking whether it is already loaded, which needs no database query, and loads only the part for a plugin that is there. On a site with none of them, not one of those files is read.

= Can I use it with a headless front end? =

Yes, with Pro. `GET /wp-json/zinn-seo/v1/head?url=/about/` returns the finished title, description, canonical address, robots, sharing tags, structured data and breadcrumbs of a page — or where the address has moved to. WPGraphQL gets a `zinnSeo` field on every post, page, term and author.

= Is the social card image made on every page view? =

No. A card is drawn once, in the background, after a post is saved, and kept as a file. A visitor's page only links to it.

= Where is my sitemap? =

At `/sitemap.xml` on your site (in no-trace mode, at a neutral address the Sitemaps and AI screen shows). robots.txt names it for every search engine; add it once in Google Search Console and Bing Webmaster Tools. If you used another SEO plugin, its sitemap address redirects to this one.

= Will a large site's sitemap slow it down or run out of memory? =

No. The sitemap is built in the background in short steps and kept as files; a request for it sends a file. A change rebuilds only the file that lists the changed page.

Images (featured, in the text, a product's gallery) are read for a whole page of posts in one query, never one at a time. The sitemap health screen shows the last build, time taken, memory used, addresses per type and anything a search engine would refuse. A readable view opens in the browser, in your language, and the addresses other SEO plugins used (sitemap_index.xml, wp-sitemap.xml) redirect to the new one.

= Should I block AI crawlers? =

Blocking the crawlers that ANSWER questions (such as OAI-SearchBot, Claude-SearchBot and PerplexityBot) removes your site from AI answers that would link to you. Blocking the ones that TRAIN models (such as GPTBot, ClaudeBot and Google-Extended) costs you nothing in search or AI answers. The AI crawlers screen offers "allow answers, block training" in one click.

= Who can change the SEO settings? =

Administrators, and anyone you give the SEO Manager role. The SEO Editor role edits the SEO fields of pages only. With Pro you can give any role any SEO permission.

= What happens to my data if I delete the plugin? =

Everything it created is removed, unless you tick "Keep my SEO data if the plugin is deleted" under Settings → Data first.

= Does the content analysis send my text anywhere? =

No. Every check runs on your own server. Only features you start reach outside: topic coverage reads the pages you name, and the AI features use the AI provider you connect (or Zinn® credits, if you choose them).

= Which languages does the readability analysis support? =

All of them. Sentence and paragraph length, subheadings and sentence variety work in every language WordPress ships. Transition words, passive voice and reading ease need each language's own rules: the Content → Languages tab shows what is measured in your site's languages.

= What can the redirect manager do? =

301, 302, 307 and 308 redirects, 410 gone and 451 unavailable for legal reasons; regular-expression patterns with $1 captures, groups, notes, hit counts, a tester that follows every hop, bulk actions, spreadsheet import and export. A changed slug, parent or category writes a redirect (a page's children move with one rule); a deleted post or product sends visitors to its parent or category, or answers 410, and publishing it again removes the rule. Older rules are pointed at a new address, so chains are never created; a check finds and fixes any already there. Import from Redirection, Yoast SEO Premium, Rank Math, All in One SEO, SEOPress, Safe Redirect Manager and .htaccess lines, with a dry run first.

= What happens when I change my permalinks or domain? =

Plan the change on the Address changes screen: you see every address that will move, the redirects are written first and the new structure is switched on only when the last one is in place. A change made on Settings → Permalinks or the command line gets its redirects straight after. Old domain names that still reach the site are sent to the same page on the new one.

= What does the hacked-site scan look for? =

Pages in Japanese or Chinese the site's language does not explain (the "Japanese keyword hack"), links hidden from visitors, pharmacy and casino spam links, scripts from unknown sites, stray sitemaps, pages that show Googlebot something different, and visitors from Google sent elsewhere. It changes nothing; it tells you what it found. Pro runs it every week and e-mails anything new.

= Will the 404 log slow my site down or fill my database? =

No. Each missing address is one counter row, not a row per visit, and the list keeps at most 10,000 addresses (the least recently seen go first). A normal page view does no extra work; a missing page adds one small update.

= Do redirects need PHP? =

They are answered before your theme loads, from small index files, so a redirect costs a fraction of a page view. With Pro they can be answered before any plugin loads, by Apache from .htaccess, or exported for Nginx, Apache and Caddy so WordPress never starts.
